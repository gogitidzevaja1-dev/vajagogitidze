01 / THE OPPORTUNITY
The Gap

MUNICH
IS MISSING
THIS

Munich has thermal baths. It has yoga studios. It has wellness spas. But it has nothing that combines premium sauna culture with a living, breathing health community — a place where curious, health-obsessed people come to learn, connect, and transform their relationship with their body.

🏛️

The Clinical Gap

Existing saunas in Munich (Therme, Müller'sches) are transactional. You pay, you sweat, you leave. No community. No education. No identity.

🧘

The Studio Gap

Yoga and mindfulness studios exist but are siloed. No heat ritual. No social culture. No cross-disciplinary health education.

✨

The Community Gap

The 20–35 health-conscious Munich demographic has no premium "home base" — a third place between work and home built around their values.

Key Risks & Mitigations

  • !

    Location: Munich's real estate market is extremely competitive. Mitigate by starting the search immediately, targeting Werksviertel or Neuhausen as well as Schwabing, and being flexible on timing.

  • !

    Complexity: Running sauna + café + events is operationally demanding. Mitigate with a phased rollout — sauna and basic café first, full programming in month 2.

  • !

    Permitting: German regulatory requirements for sauna build-outs are significant. Mitigate by engaging a specialist architect and planning consultant from day one.
