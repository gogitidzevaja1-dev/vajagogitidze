The Brand

What is ANY DI SunCover?

ANY DI is a Munich-based luxury accessories brand founded by designer Anne Dickhardt in 2015. Their signature product — the design-patented SunCover — is a revolutionary slim eyeglass cover that doubles as a stylish bag tag. Crafted with vegan leather, 24-carat gold-plated hardware, and soft microfiber lining, it protects all shapes and sizes of glasses while looking stunning.

Import & Logistics

Getting Product From Munich to Albania

Customs & Duties

  • Albania's SAA with the EU = preferential / 0% tariff on industrial products from EU
  • Luxury accessories may fall 0%–15% depending on HS code classification
  • VAT at 20% on CIF value (cost + insurance + freight)
  • Customs scanning fee: €22 for declarations over €1,000

Key advantage: EU-origin goods benefit from significantly reduced or zero customs duties under Albania's SAA trade agreement.

Shipping Options

  • Road freight (best): Munich → Durres via Austria, Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro. 3–5 days.
  • Groupage/LTL: GALA, LKW Walter, Balkan Trans handle Germany–Albania routes regularly.
  • Parcel: DHL/UPS for small initial orders. Simple but higher per-unit cost.
  • Sea freight: Hamburg/Bari → Port of Durres. Economical for bulk.

Legal Setup

Registering Your Albanian Company

The Albanian LLC (SHPK) — fast, online, affordable. Typically completed in 1–2 business days.

01

Reserve Name

Check at National Business Center (QKB). Takes 1 day.

02

Prepare Docs

Articles of association, notarized & translated. Min capital: 100 LEK.

03

Register Online

Submit via e-Albania. Approval within 48h. Certificate in 24h after.

04

Tax & Bank

Get NIPT (tax ID), register VAT, open bank account within 20 days.

05

Go Fiscal

Electronic fiscal cert (~4,000 LEK/yr), e-signature (~4,800 LEK/yr), stamp (~3,000 LEK).

Total registration cost: approx. €200–€400. Corporate tax: 15%. VAT: 20%.
